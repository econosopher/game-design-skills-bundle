# Big Five Personality Audit

Adapted from Stanislav Stankovic's named design lens. Unsupported outcome claims and prescriptive psychological judgments have been removed.

## OCEAN without invented psychometrics

The named dimensions are Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness and Neuroticism. Preserve those names when discussing the framework. This skill supplies no instrument, scoring model or validated mapping from mechanics to traits.

Inspect design demands directly: novelty and ambiguity; planning and routine; social intensity; cooperation and conflict; uncertainty, failure cost and recovery. These are observable design variables. The former trait-to-genre preference tables have been removed because they were not supported by an identified study in the bundle.

Do not label a player neurotic because they avoid losses, agreeable because they cooperate, or extraverted because they join a guild. Such actions can follow payoffs, information, skill and constraints. If validated trait measures and a suitable research design are provided, evaluate their incremental explanatory or predictive value beyond those alternatives, including out-of-sample performance and uncertainty.

Without such evidence, return design-fit hypotheses, not trait diagnoses or retention forecasts. Do not manufacture an OCEAN analysis when the user only needs behavioral segmentation.
