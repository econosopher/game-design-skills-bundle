# Systematizing Empathizing Audit

Adapted from Stanislav Stankovic's named design lens. Unsupported outcome claims and prescriptive psychological judgments have been removed.

## Two design axes, not two human types

Retain the upstream adaptation as a descriptive comparison. Systematizing: how much success rewards learning rules, predicting outcomes and optimizing decisions. Empathizing, in this bundle's adapted terminology: how the design communicates consequences, supports expression and handles failure or recovery. This second axis is not a psychometric empathy measure.

Inspect rule consistency, available information, strategic tradeoffs, feedback, failure costs, accessibility, social consequences and recovery choices. The axes need not trade off. Qualitative placement is a heuristic, not a measured latent trait.

The four high/low combinations describe those two sets of affordances only. Remove the inference that a low/low quadrant causes weak retention, or a high/high quadrant produces loyalty. Likewise remove fixed optimizer, cozy and social persona preference tables. Assess observed preferences under the actual constraints instead.

A demanding design can be appropriate for its audience; low-pressure play can also be valuable. Recommend a change only when the stated objective and evidence justify its costs.
