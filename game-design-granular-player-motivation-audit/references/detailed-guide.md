# Granular motivation review

Use [the retained vocabulary](granular-taxonomy-notes.md) for the ten motivation themes. Match the specificity of the hypothesis to the observed behavior. Compare status and rank with team leadership, authorship with narrative absorption, and affiliation with coordinated competitive success. Multiple motives can coexist and change by context.

Do not infer shares, demographics, fixed identities or retention effects from these labels. The bundle provides no validated classifier. Describe the actual choices, outcomes and constraints, then propose a segment definition that can be tested with player data. Report overlap and uncertainty; avoid declaring an audience impossible to design or market for.

Present the mechanism, supplied evidence, competing explanation and implication for the current decision. A claimed cognitive benefit is a separate empirical claim, not a conclusion established by a motivation category.
