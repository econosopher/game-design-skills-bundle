# Game Design Granular Player Motivation Audit

Review audience fit with the bundled granular motivation taxonomy when broader archetypes cannot distinguish the relevant player motivations.

## Result

- **Audit target** - what is being reviewed and what it is supposed to do
- **Motivation-theme reading** - which of the 10 themes the design activates
- **Archetype-by-archetype reading** - how each player lens experiences the design
- **Segment profile** - which archetypes are strongly served, weakly served, or actively frustrated
- **Mismatch diagnosis** - where the design overcommits, neglects, or creates motivational conflict
- **Recommendations** - what to strengthen, soften, separate, or clarify
