# Social Satisfaction Matrix

Adapted from Stanislav Stankovic's named design lens. Unsupported outcome claims and prescriptive psychological judgments have been removed.

## Retained four-quadrant heuristic

Use the upstream names as a discussion matrix. Basic: low satisfaction upside, high dissatisfaction downside. Critical: high on both. Booster: high upside, low downside. Secondary: low on both. Define what high and low mean for the target audience and mark unknown placements as hypotheses.

No feature belongs to a quadrant universally. A friend list is not automatically Basic, voice chat Critical or gifting a Booster. Identify the behavior, interaction frequency, counterpart incentives, coordination benefit, conflict or abuse opportunity, substitutes and implementation cost. Distinguish the costs of presence, absence and poor implementation.

Ground satisfaction claims in suitable player evidence. Track commercial effects separately. Classifying a feature does not quantify either. Select priorities using expected impact, affected audience, uncertainty, dependencies and cost; do not prescribe Basic-before-Booster or any other quadrant ordering.

Possible changes include adding, removing, modifying or bundling features. Explain the relevant mechanism and comparison, including when doing nothing is preferable.
