# Player Need Satisfaction Audit

Adapted from Stanislav Stankovic's named design lens. Unsupported outcome claims and prescriptive psychological judgments have been removed.

## Choice, competence and relationships

Keep the upstream autonomy, competence and relatedness vocabulary as questions about reported experience. Autonomy concerns perceived volition; competence concerns effectiveness or mastery; relatedness concerns connection with others. Do not equate the number of choices with autonomy or the presence of chat with relatedness.

Inspect actual alternatives, clarity of consequences, learning feedback, skill demands and the incentives governing interaction. Ask what players report and how behavior responds when one constraint changes. A feature can serve one audience while imposing costs on another. Do not prescribe equal coverage of all three dimensions.

The [2006 SDT game studies](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf) provide relevant evidence about enjoyment and preferences, not a license to infer long-term churn from a checklist. State the population, design and outcome before transporting a result.

Replace judgments such as nourishing, depleting or healthy with either an explicitly requested experience criterion or an observable prediction. Recommend changes based on expected benefit, opportunity cost and evidence.
