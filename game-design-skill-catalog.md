# Game Design Skill Catalog

A quick index of the game design skills currently available in the workspace.

## Emotional / thematic direction

### game-design-emotional-canvas
Define a game concept's emotional identity and sensory direction when preparing an emotional brief or aligning art, audio and narrative.

### emotional-canvas-moodboard
Create a curated HTML and JPG moodboard from a game emotional canvas or an established emotional direction.

## Beginner planning / early production strategy

### game-dev-first-steps
Turn a beginner game concept into a feasible first prototype and build order using the team's skills and constraints.

### game-dev-team-gap
Identify missing game-development capabilities and the smallest workable team for a specified concept and milestone.

### game-dev-budget-estimator
Estimate a game milestone's budget range from scope, team coverage, geography and work model when planning cost or reducing spend.

### game-dev-time-estimator
Estimate a game milestone's schedule range from scope, dependencies and team availability when testing a deadline or planning delivery.

## General decision framework

### game-design-grow-design
Use Goal, Reality, Options and Will to resolve a game-design decision when the team needs a structured path from ambiguity to commitment.

## Motivation / psychology audits

### game-design-player-motivation-audit
Review stated reasons, incentives and observed participation; use the SDT motivation vocabulary only as hypotheses when it helps a player-motivation question.

### game-design-player-need-satisfaction-audit
Inspect player choice, mastery feedback and relationships when a need-satisfaction lens is requested; distinguish reported experience from inferred outcomes.

### game-design-bartle-archetype-audit
Review audience fit through Bartle motivation lenses when comparing Achievers, Explorers, Socializers and Killers (competition, domination, disruption and impact on others).

### game-design-granular-player-motivation-audit
Compare detailed motivation hypotheses when broad archetypes miss distinctions such as status versus leadership or authorship versus story immersion.

### game-design-big-five-personality-audit
Use an explicitly requested OCEAN lens to examine player-fit hypotheses, without inferring personality, audience shares or product outcomes from mechanics.

## Multiplayer / social-system audits

### game-design-multiplayer-feature-audit
Review social-system fit, coordination costs and synchronization when evaluating multiplayer, guild or community features.

## Prototyping / uncertainty

### game-design-unknown-unknowns-prototyping
Map design uncertainty and choose inexpensive learning prototypes before committing an underdefined concept to production.

## GROW-derived workflow family

### game-design-goal-framing
Turn a vague feature idea into a goal with purpose, game fit, success criteria and constraints before selecting a solution.

### game-design-option-generation
Generate credible alternative solutions and compare tradeoffs when a game-design problem has no selected approach yet.

### game-design-five-options
Develop and compare five game-design approaches when the user wants a deliberately broader option set before choosing.

### game-design-roadblock-reframing
Generate feasible routes around a specific design or production blocker by temporarily removing it and examining alternatives.

### game-design-ideal-outcome-backcasting
Work backward from a desired player experience to its enabling conditions and near-term design priorities.

### game-design-transformative-reuse
Find ways to adapt, recombine or retune existing game systems when solving a design problem with limited production capacity.

### game-design-feature-prioritization
Rank existing game-feature options by expected impact, cost, strategic fit and timing when choosing what to build next.

## Behavior / feature-adoption audits

### game-design-fogg-behavior-audit
Diagnose feature adoption through Motivation, Ability and Prompt when a specific intended player action is not happening.

## Randomness / fairness-perception audits

### game-design-perceived-randomness-audit
Review perceived randomness, streaks and decision timing when players mistrust chance mechanics or feel their choices are undermined.

### game-design-attribution-audit
Diagnose how players explain success or failure, using locus, stability and controllability when blame or teachability is the question.

### game-design-flow-audit
Review challenge and skill over time when diagnosing boredom, anxiety, pacing spikes or breaks in player absorption.

### game-design-fairness-frustration-audit
Diagnose perceived unfairness by combining attribution, randomness and challenge-skill alignment when players feel cheated or resist retrying.

### game-design-failure-loop-audit
Review loss, learning, recovery and re-entry when tuning retries or diagnosing what happens after player failure.

## Fantasy / audience / structure extractors

### game-design-fantasy-extractor
Extract the player fantasy from supported actions and experiences when a concept names features but lacks an emotional promise.

### game-design-player-persona-extractor
Infer a behavior-first player persona and anti-persona from the design when clarifying audience fit and unsupported demographic assumptions.

### game-design-design-pillars-extractor
Extract actionable design pillars from mechanics and tradeoffs when stated pillars are vague, generic or contradictory.

### game-design-core-loop-extractor
Extract repeated player actions, feedback, rewards and renewed motivation when a game concept lacks a clear core loop.

### game-design-molyneux-lens
Stress-test a game concept's ambition and reactivity with bold provocations, then identify the smallest feasible version worth prototyping.

### game-design-thinking-fast-and-slow-audit
Review intuitive versus deliberate cognitive demands when pacing, overload or transitions between thinking modes are in question.

### game-design-peak-end-audit
Review emotional peaks and endings when investigating how a session, feature or event is likely to be remembered.

### game-design-zeigarnik-effect-audit
Examine unfinished tasks, recall and resumption when open loops are at issue; test these distinct outcomes instead of assuming an unfinished-task effect.

## FTUE / onboarding audits

### game-design-ftue-hero-journey-audit
Review onboarding through the Hero's Journey lens when the question is first-session emotional engagement and narrative progression.

## Session structure / retention audits

### game-design-goal-density-and-immediacy-audit
Review session goals, time horizons, closure and return triggers when players struggle to find something worthwhile to do.

### game-design-friction-journey-audit
Map accumulated friction along a player journey when distinguishing useful challenge from obstruction or dependency deadlock.

### game-design-player-values-mapper
Infer possible player values from observed behavior when translating motivational priorities into design implications.

### game-design-player-perspective-reframe
Reframe how a player reads an existing game state when the obstacle is interpretation rather than an actual progression block.

### game-design-social-satisfaction-matrix
Map social features by supported satisfaction upside and dissatisfaction downside; prioritize using audience, expected impact and cost rather than quadrant alone.

### game-design-prosocial-session-chapters
Design the before, during and after phases of a multiplayer session when improving cooperation, social learning and continuity.

## Pitch / publishing audits

### game-design-pitch-deck-audit
Review a game pitch deck for clarity, evidence, readability and publisher fit before a funding or publishing conversation.

### game-design-one-page-design-doc
Create a one-page game concept document as Markdown, structured JSON and PDF when a compact design handoff is requested.

## Creative disruption / unblockers

### game-design-creative-unblocker
Use oblique prompts and sideways reframing when game-design ideation is stuck or familiar options feel stale.

## Adversarial / red-team audits

### design-red-team-audit
Stress-test a game proposal through a pre-mortem when the user wants failure modes, weak assumptions and ways to reduce risk.

## Project-adjacent collaboration analysis

### software-team-conflict-patterns
Analyze recurring software-team collaboration problems using observable actions, incentives and project consequences; suggest responses without personality diagnoses.

## Suggested ways to combine them

- **Need clarity first** -> `game-design-goal-framing`
- **Need more solution paths** -> `game-design-option-generation` or `game-design-five-options`
- **Need to get unstuck** -> `game-design-roadblock-reframing` or `game-design-creative-unblocker`
- **Need stronger emotional direction** -> `game-design-emotional-canvas` or `game-design-fantasy-extractor`
- **Need better psychological diagnosis** -> `game-design-player-motivation-audit`, `game-design-player-need-satisfaction-audit`, `game-design-attribution-audit`, or `game-design-flow-audit`
- **Need to decide what to prototype** -> `game-design-unknown-unknowns-prototyping`
- **Need to pick what to do now** -> `game-design-feature-prioritization`
- **Need to clarify who the game is really for** -> `game-design-player-persona-extractor`
- **Need to extract the real design principles** -> `game-design-design-pillars-extractor`
- **Need to isolate the actual repeatable structure** -> `game-design-core-loop-extractor`
- **Need to understand why failure feels bad or good** -> `game-design-failure-loop-audit` or `game-design-fairness-frustration-audit`
- **Need a provocative ambition check** -> `game-design-molyneux-lens`
- **Need to audit cognitive demand and mode-switching** -> `game-design-thinking-fast-and-slow-audit`
- **Need to audit remembered aftertaste and dominant moments** -> `game-design-peak-end-audit`
- **Need to audit unresolved-task tension and return pull** -> `game-design-zeigarnik-effect-audit`
- **Need a beginner planning sequence** -> `game-dev-first-steps` -> `game-dev-team-gap` -> `game-dev-budget-estimator` -> `game-dev-time-estimator`
- **Need a deadline realism check** -> `game-dev-time-estimator`

## Notes

- The GROW-derived skills are mostly workflow skills.
- The motivation and need-satisfaction skills are mostly audit skills.
- `game-design-creative-unblocker` is intentionally sillier and more disruptive than the rest of the pack.

### game-design-brainstorm-methods
Choose and apply a named brainstorming method to a game-design problem when structured ideation is requested.

### game-design-craft-critique
Analyze a playable reference game using Raph Koster's layered craft method, comparing systems, experience and inferred intent.

### game-design-kpi-coverage-audit
Review whether a feature's KPI framing captures its intended value when measurement blind spots could distort prioritization.

### game-design-leaderboard-audit
Audit an existing or proposed leaderboard for stagnation, scale, goals, reward brackets, group composition and top-of-ladder behavior.

### game-design-leaderboard-builder
Design a leaderboard specification when its ranking, grouping, rewards, cadence and competitive progression need to be defined.

### game-design-novelty-spectrum-audit
Review familiarity, novelty and expectation clashes when a game concept may be too derivative or too difficult to understand.

### game-design-one-thing-to-remove
Identify the highest-value subtraction from a game design when the user asks what to cut or simplify.

### game-design-player-segment-perception-audit
Compare how experience, tenure, spending and social context change feature perception when one generic player view is inadequate.

### game-design-premium-pass-audit
Audit a battle or season pass for player value, attainable progression, reward pacing, free-track effects and incremental economy outcomes.

### game-design-prototype-intent-audit
Check whether a prototype tests the intended uncertainty when scope, fidelity or evaluation could invalidate its learning purpose.

### game-design-prototyping-companion
Record prototype branches, evidence, dead ends and revisit conditions; optionally render an SVG map of the experiment history.

### game-design-smart-goal-audit
Audit or rewrite game-design objectives against SMART criteria when success conditions are unclear or need stress-testing.

### game-design-systematizing-empathizing-audit
Compare rule-learning demands with experience support when the user requests the systematizing/empathizing design lens; do not classify human psychology.

### moodboard-generator
Create a themed HTML and PNG visual moodboard when the user wants curated visual references beyond a game emotional-canvas workflow.
