# Software team conflict patterns

Separate one incident from a recurring pattern and distinguish observed actions from inferred motives. Inspect ownership, incentives, deadlines, information and decision rights. Do not diagnose people, presume malice, or prescribe retaliation or humiliation.

Use [the detailed guide](references/detailed-guide.md) for evidence and response choices. Source archetype names are optional shorthand, not a requirement or a psychometric assessment. Return a proportionate action and the evidence that could change it.
