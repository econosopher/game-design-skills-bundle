# Software Team Conflict Patterns

Adapted from Stanislav Stankovic's design lens.

Describe the concrete behavior, repetition, project consequence and sources. Separate first-hand evidence from one participant's interpretation. Ask whether workload, unclear ownership, conflicting incentives, missing information or decision rights explain the pattern.

Compare practical options: clarify a decision, document acceptance criteria, change a handoff, assign ownership, adjust scope, coach a skill or escalate an evidenced concern through an appropriate channel. Account for authority, power imbalance and retaliation risk without assuming these are present.

Do not infer ego, malice, incompetence, mental illness or a fixed character from an archetype label. Do not give numerical fix probabilities without data or recommend firing as an automatic response to a taxonomy. The [source vocabulary](neil-on-software-archetypes.md) is retained for attribution, not to recreate unsupported narratives.

A useful answer identifies what is known, alternative explanations, an actionable next step and a condition for revisiting the conclusion.
