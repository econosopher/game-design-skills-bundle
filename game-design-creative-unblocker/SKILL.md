# Game Design Creative Unblocker

Use oblique prompts and sideways reframing when game-design ideation is stuck or familiar options feel stale.

## Result

- **Stuck point** - what feels blocked, muddy, stale, or overworked
- **Oblique prompts** - 1 to 3 chosen prompts
- **Prompt interpretations** - what each prompt suggests in this context
- **Sideways directions** - surprising design directions, edits, cuts, or experiments
- **Salvageable next step** - the most useful thing to test, discuss, or prototype next
