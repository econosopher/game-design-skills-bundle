# Approved substantive dispositions

The fork maintainer approved these dispositions on 2026-09-13. This public register records shareable changes and their scope. The full evidence/profile register remains private. Approval determines the fork's instructions; it does not establish whether an empirical claim is true.

| IDs | Disposition | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E01-E03 | Remove | Remove universal currency-return, discount and breakeven-tier judgments, plus the asserted obligation-to-churn causal rule. Retain accounting and evaluation of actual rewards, time costs and alternatives. |
| E04-E05, E09 | Keep | Retain all substantive leaderboard guidance, including the elite-coverage claim, reward thresholds and intrinsic/extrinsic retention rules. The explicit instruction to retain everything on leaderboards governs E09 as well. |
| E06 | Qualify | Evaluate permanent exclusivity, early access and later availability as alternative policies. |
| E07 | Replace | Compare optional skip prices with marginal time saved and demand. Do not infer designer intent from the ratio to entry price. |
| E08 | Replace | Estimate pass completion by purchaser segment, entry date and XP opportunity; remove the universal available-playtime percentage. |
| P01 | Replace | Remove undefined wellness judgments and automatic motivation-category forecasts. Keep SDT vocabulary as hypotheses and distinguish reported experience from commercial outcomes. |
| P02 | Replace | Separate unfinished-task recall, resumption and retention; remove automatic memory and coercion claims. |
| P03 | Replace | Remove unsupported trait-to-game predictions, audience-share numbers and fixed persona narratives. Retain named vocabularies and require evidence for segmentation or prediction. |
| P04 | Replace | Retain the four social-satisfaction quadrants as a heuristic; remove automatic feature classifications and priority order. |

The approved removal of unsupported psychology also covers deterministic peak-end and flow claims, moralized attribution, unconditional randomness rankings, overconfident value inference and unsupported personnel predictions. Useful distinctions remain: challenge versus skill, experience versus recall, locus/stability/controllability, randomness before versus after choice, and observed behavior versus inferred motives. Named methods retain their identities; a framework label supplies no effect size or diagnosis.

Relevant evidence includes [Bartle's original MUD taxonomy](https://mud.co.uk/richard/hcds.htm), which describes its limited origin; [the 2006 SDT game studies](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf), which provide context-specific evidence rather than a universal commercial rule; and [the 2025 interruption meta-analysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-05000-w), which distinguishes recall from resumption. Supported behavioral findings remain usable. Incentives and constraints are the starting point, and contrary evidence can change the recommendation.

The separately authored economy skill evaluates leaderboards against their intended objectives. The retained leaderboard audit has a more prescriptive view. This difference is intentional under the approved scope; do not silently treat both as the same rule or claim that maintainer approval resolves the empirical disagreement.
