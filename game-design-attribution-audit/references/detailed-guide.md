# Attribution Audit

Adapted from Stanislav Stankovic's design lens.

Retain three distinctions: locus (internal or external explanation), stability (persistent or temporary cause) and controllability (what the player can affect). These classify explanations, not their truth or moral quality.

For a concrete success or failure, inspect the actual rules, inputs, uncertainty and available feedback. Compare what happened with the evidence shown to players and with their stated explanations. A server failure, hidden rule or impossible matchup may make external attribution correct. Do not train the player to accept personal blame for a genuine product fault.

Identify missing information, ambiguous feedback, unlearnable variance or misleading presentation. Separate disagreement about fairness from factual error. Claims about learned helplessness, toxicity or churn require additional evidence and must not be inferred from an attribution label.

Recommend a change to the underlying mechanism or its explanation. State the expected learning or behavioral effect, comparison and uncertainty.
