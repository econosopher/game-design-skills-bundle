# Game Design Fantasy Extractor

Extract the player fantasy from supported actions and experiences when a concept names features but lacks an emotional promise.

## Design lens

Players do not attach to mechanics in the abstract. They attach to what those mechanics let them be.

A useful fantasy statement does three things:
- names who the player becomes
- implies what emotional payoff they are chasing
- is supported by repeated gameplay, not just theme dressing

If the fantasy could describe fifty unrelated games, it is too weak to guide design.

## Result

- **Core fantasy** - the strongest player-facing fantasy implied by the design
- **Supporting fantasy layers** - secondary fantasies or emotional promises present in the design
- **Fantasy support map** - which systems reinforce the fantasy and which fail to do so
- **Fantasy conflicts** - where systems, pacing, or progression undermine the claimed fantasy
- **Design implications** - what must be strengthened, clarified, cut, or re-framed
