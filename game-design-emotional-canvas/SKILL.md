# Game Design Emotional Canvas

Define a game concept's emotional identity and sensory direction when preparing an emotional brief or aligning art, audio and narrative.

## Result

a compact emotional canvas with these outputs:

- **Target feeling** - the dominant emotional promise
- **Emotional field** - adjacent and opposing feelings
- **Emotional palette** - sensory and aesthetic ingredients that reinforce the feeling
- **Anti-patterns** - choices that weaken or break the mood
- **Design heuristics** - short creative principles for future reviews
- **Moodboard brief** - a handoff-ready summary for adjacent disciplines
