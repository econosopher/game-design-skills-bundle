# Game Design FTUE Hero's Journey Audit

Review onboarding through the Hero's Journey lens when the question is first-session emotional engagement and narrative progression.

## Design lens

The purpose of FTUE is not merely to explain controls.
The deeper purpose is to create an emotional connection and make the player feel like the hero of their own story.

Use the bundled four-part adaptation: broad framing, Call to Adventure, Meeting the Mentor and Crossing the Threshold. Read the guide for stage-specific checks rather than substituting the full monomyth.

## Result

- **FTUE scope** - what span of the experience is being audited
- **Hero's Journey stage review** - strengths and weaknesses by stage
- **Psychological need check** - autonomy, competence, relatedness where relevant
- **Drop-risk diagnosis** - where the FTUE is likely to lose players
- **Recommendations** - what to cut, pace, reframe, or strengthen
