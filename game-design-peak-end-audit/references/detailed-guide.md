# Peak End Audit

Adapted from Stanislav Stankovic's design lens.

Define the experience window and audience. Map momentary experience, salient positive or negative moments, the ending and the delay before recall. Distinguish remembered evaluation from in-session reports, repeat play and purchases.

The peak-end lens suggests a hypothesis about weighting of salient moments and endings. This bundle provides no calibrated game-specific weighting model. Compare that hypothesis with average quality, duration, accumulated rewards, recency and selection effects. Identify evidence and uncertainty instead of asserting which moment will dominate memory.

When evaluating a revised ending, hold other important features stable where possible and measure both experience and later choices. A better final impression does not justify ignoring costs incurred earlier. Recommend changing, preserving or removing a moment only with a mechanism tied to the actual goal.
