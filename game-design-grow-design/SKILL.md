# Game Design GROW Design

Use Goal, Reality, Options and Will to resolve a game-design decision when the team needs a structured path from ambiguity to commitment.

## Result

- **Goal** - a concise statement of the intended outcome and success criteria
- **Reality** - the current state, constraints, dependencies, and unknowns
- **Options** - multiple credible paths, not just one preferred answer
- **Will** - a recommendation, immediate next steps, and validation needs
