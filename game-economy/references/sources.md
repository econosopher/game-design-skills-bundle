# Public sources

These articles by Phillip Black inform the methods. Each entry states the idea used, not endorsement of every historical claim or formula in the article. URLs were reachable on 2026-09-13.

- [Optimal Area Currency With Milton](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/optimal-area-currency-with-milton/) (2016-01-05): Currency segmentation and substitution; use the mechanism rather than historical game mappings.

- [How to Measure Whales](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/how-to-measure-whales/) (2016-05-21): Revenue concentration across explicitly defined populations; no universal payer segmentation cutoff.

- [The Economics of Battle Pass Are Broken. Let’s Fix It.](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/the-economics-of-battle-pass-are-broken-lets-fix-it/) (2021-04-11): ADMC, optional spend depth and marginal time pricing; a spend ceiling is not a revenue forecast.

- [More on the Economics of Battle Pass: Resting Prices, Forecasted Level and Complete Pass](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/more-on-the-economics-of-battle-pass-resting-prices-forecasted-level-and-complete-pass/) (2021-05-09): Late-entry value, catch-up design and catalog access.

- [Why Virtual Currency Exists](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/why-virtual-currency-exists/) (2021-06-05): Transaction friction and currency use cases; historical platform details require fresh verification.

- [Why I’m a Supply-Side Game Economist](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/why-im-a-supply-side-game-economist/) (2021-06-27): Production costs, cadence and the feasible quality/quantity frontier.

- [Supply Chains In Everything](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/supply-chains-in-everything/) (2023-06-07): How system design changes content production bottlenecks.

- [WTF Are These Three Battle Pass Things](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/wtf-are-these-three-battle-pass-things/) (2024-11-17): Pass cadence, price architecture and time/money economics.

- [Fisharian Game Economy Tradition](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/fisharian-game-economy-tradition/) (2025-02-17): Explicit resource flows and reproducible economic models.

- [The Economics of Cannibalization & Royal Kingdom](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/the-economics-of-cannibalization-royal-kingdom/) (2025-04-16): Incremental portfolio value after displacement.

- [The System Designer’s Jawbreaker](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/the-system-designers-jawbreaker/) (2025-08-21): Labor/capital substitution and reward-source placement.

- [Player Empathy, The East, and Dirty, Dirty Games](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/player-empathy-the-east-and-dirty-dirty-games/) (2025-11-20): Respecting revealed preferences rather than imposing designer tastes; historical market comparisons are not adopted as laws.

- [The Streak Secret Squad Busters Doesn't Want You to Know](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/the-streak-secret-squad-busters-doesnt-want-you-to-know/) (2026-01-13): Distinguishing diminishing marginal utility from reference-dependent explanations.

- [Minimum Falsifiable Product for Experiments or You're Stupid.](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/minimum-falsifiable-product-for-experiments-or-youre-stupid/) (2026-02-13): Predefined falsification conditions and actionability.

- [What Merge-2 Economics Is Missing](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/more-merge-2-economics/) (2026-03-25): Expected base-unit output and generator efficiency under stated assumptions.

- [The Economics of Merge-2 Games](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/the-economics-of-merge-games/) (2026-03-25): Resource, time and board constraints; mathematical approximations need explicit units and finite-system caveats.

- [If I pressed for the definition of "progression" in games, what would you give?](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/if-i-pressed-for-the-definition-of-progression-in-games-what-would-you-give/) (2026-04-12): Progression as the unfolding of systems, without presupposing linearity.

- [The Wage Theory of Game Progression](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/the-wage-theory-of-game-progression/) (2026-05-07): Earned progression per unit of engagement and the content-exhaustion tradeoff.

- [No. 1 Game Experimentation Mistake](https://gameeconomistconsulting.com/no-1-game-experimentation-mistake/) (2026-08-17): Realized treatment, reach, duration and net additional activity.
