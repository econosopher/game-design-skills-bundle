# Game Design Multiplayer Feature Audit

Review social-system fit, coordination costs and synchronization when evaluating multiplayer, guild or community features.

## Design lens

Social design is not a checklist of features.

A leaderboard, guild, chat channel, or PvP mode does not automatically create meaningful social play. Strong multiplayer design aligns player motivation, time structure, coordination demands, visibility, and community purpose.

## Result

- **Audit target** - what is being reviewed and what kind of social experience it appears to aim for
- **Social promise** - the core social fantasy or player promise
- **Motivation map** - competition, collaboration, collaborate-to-compete, belonging, vanity/status, knowledge exchange
- **Time and synchronization audit** - realtime, non-realtime, synchronous, asynchronous, or hybrid
- **Social depth audit** - how deep the interaction really goes
- **Community and status audit** - whether the system supports durable groups, identity, and readable prestige
- **Risks / failure modes** - where the design is likely to break, flatten, or create friction
- **Recommendations** - what to strengthen, stage, simplify, avoid, or postpone
