# Game Design Leaderboard Audit

Audit an existing or proposed leaderboard for stagnation, scale, goals, reward brackets, group composition and top-of-ladder behavior.

## Design lens

A leaderboard is the start of a metagame. Its quality is not measured by whether it ranks players — it is measured by whether it generates meaningful, emotionally resonant competition for the majority of the player base, from the player at the very top to the player sitting in the middle of a group of 100.

## Result

- **Audit target** — what is being reviewed and what context it sits in
- **Stagnation audit** — whether the leaderboard feels alive or frozen
- **Scale audit** — whether the numbers are human-meaningful
- **Goal density audit** — how many distinct goals the structure generates
- **Motivation audit** — the quality and balance of intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation
- **Reward bracket audit** — coverage, fairness, and value calibration
- **Group composition audit** — fairness and competitive integrity
- **Top-of-ladder audit** — what happens when a player reaches the ceiling
- **Failure patterns** — named anti-patterns present in the design
- **Recommendations** — prioritized, specific, actionable
