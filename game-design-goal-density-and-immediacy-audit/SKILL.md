# Game Design Goal Density and Immediacy Audit

Review session goals, time horizons, closure and return triggers when players struggle to find something worthwhile to do.

## Design lens

A strong session structure does not depend on one perfect goal.
It gives the player enough meaningful goals of different sizes and time horizons that they can pick one that fits the time, energy, and attention they currently have.

## Result

- **Audit target** - what is being reviewed and what kind of session pattern it supports
- **Goal density audit** - how many meaningful goals are available at once and how varied they are
- **Goal immediacy audit** - how quickly the player can identify something worth doing now
- **Time-horizon breakdown** - short-term, mid-term, and long-term goals
- **Session closure audit** - whether the player can leave at a safe, satisfying moment
- **Return-trigger audit** - what unfinished business pulls the player back
- **Recommendations** - what to add, remove, clarify, pace, or restructure
